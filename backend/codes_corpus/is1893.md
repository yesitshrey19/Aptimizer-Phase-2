IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 — Criteria for Earthquake Resistant Design of Structures, General Provisions and Buildings (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 (Sixth Revision), checked by Saurav Ambasta in Phase II testing against the PDF copy of the standard. Page numbers are printed page numbers. This is not the text of the Code. The 2002 edition's values are in is1893_2002.md.

Table 3 Seismic zone factor Z
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Table 3, the seismic zone factor Z is 0.10 for zone II, 0.16 for zone III, 0.24 for zone IV and 0.36 for zone V. Z is used in the design horizontal seismic coefficient of Cl. 6.4.2. Checked on printed page 10.

Annex E Zone factors for some important towns
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Annex E, Bengaluru (Bangalore) is in seismic zone II (Z = 0.10), Chennai is in zone III (Z = 0.16) and Delhi is in zone IV (Z = 0.24). Checked on printed page 36.

6.4.2 Design horizontal seismic coefficient
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Cl. 6.4.2, the design horizontal seismic coefficient is Ah = (Z/2) × (I/R) × (Sa/g), where Z is the zone factor of Table 3, I the importance factor, R the response reduction factor and Sa/g the design acceleration coefficient. Checked on printed page 9.

7.6.2 Approximate fundamental natural period
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Cl. 7.6.2, the approximate fundamental translational natural period is (a) Ta = 0.075 h^0.75 seconds for a bare reinforced concrete moment-resisting frame building without masonry infills, and (c) Ta = 0.09 h / √d seconds for buildings with masonry infills, where h is the building height and d the base dimension along the direction of shaking, in metres. Checked on printed page 21.

7.6.1 Design base shear
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Cl. 7.6.1, the design base shear along any principal direction of a building is VB = Ah × W, where Ah is from Cl. 6.4.2 using the approximate period Ta of Cl. 7.6.2 and W is the seismic weight of the building. Checked on printed page 21.

Table 8 Importance factor I
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Table 8, the importance factor is 1.5 for important service and community buildings such as hospitals, schools and emergency buildings; 1.2 for residential or commercial buildings with an occupancy of more than 200 persons; and 1.0 for all other buildings, which includes an ordinary residential building. Checked on printed page 19.

7.2.4 Damping ratio
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Cl. 7.2.4, damping is taken as 5 percent of critical for estimating Ah in the design lateral force of a building, whatever the material of construction (steel, reinforced concrete, masonry or a combination). Checked on printed page 19.

Table 9 Response reduction factor R
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Table 9, the response reduction factor R is 3.0 for reinforced concrete buildings with an ordinary moment resisting frame (OMRF) and 5.0 for reinforced concrete buildings with a special moment resisting frame (SMRF). Checked on printed page 20.

Table 10 Percentage of imposed load in seismic weight
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Table 10, the seismic weight includes 25 percent of the imposed floor load where the uniformly distributed imposed load is up to and including 3.0 kN/m², and 50 percent where it is above 3.0 kN/m². Checked on printed page 20.

7.3.2 Imposed load on the roof
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Cl. 7.3.2, the imposed load on the roof need not be considered when calculating design seismic forces, although the weights of equipment and other permanently fixed facilities on the roof should be included. Checked on printed page 20.

7.11.1.1 Storey drift limitation
Under IS 1893 (Part 1):2016 Cl. 7.11.1.1, the storey drift in any storey shall not exceed 0.004 times the storey height under the design base shear, with the partial safety factor for all loads taken as 1.0. Checked on printed page 26.

1.1.1 Where ductile detailing is required
Under IS 13920:1993 Cl. 1.1.1 (a) to (d), ductile detailing is required in seismic zones IV and V for all reinforced concrete structures; in zone III it is required only when the importance factor I is greater than 1.0, or the structure is an industrial structure, or the structure is more than 5 storeys high.

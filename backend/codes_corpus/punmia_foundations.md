24.2 Minimum depth of foundation by Rankine's formula
Rankine's formula gives the minimum depth of a foundation as Df = (q/γ) × ((1 − sin φ)/(1 + sin φ))², where q is the gross bearing pressure on the soil, γ the unit weight of the soil and φ its angle of shearing resistance. It is Punmia's Equation 24.8 in Section 24.2, page 677.

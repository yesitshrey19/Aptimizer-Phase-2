NBC 2016 Part 3 — Development Control Rules and General Building Requirements (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from NBC 2016 Volume 1, checked page by page in Phase II testing. Page numbers are the printed page numbers of Part 3. This is not the text of the Code. NBC 2016 has since been replaced by SP 7:2026; these are the NBC 2016 values.

12.2.1 Height of habitable rooms
In NBC 2016, every room used for human habitation (living room, bedroom) shall have a height of not less than 2.75 m, measured from the surface of the floor to the lowest point of the ceiling, that is the bottom of the slab. For pitched roofs the average height shall be not less than 2.75 m. Checked on page 43 of Part 3.

12.2.2 Size of habitable rooms
In NBC 2016, where a dwelling has only one habitable room, its area shall be not less than 9.5 m² with a minimum width of 2.4 m. Where there are two habitable rooms, one shall be not less than 9.5 m² and the other not less than 7.5 m², with a minimum width of 2.1 m. Checked on page 44 of Part 3.

12.3.2 Size of kitchens
In NBC 2016, a kitchen in a residential building where a separate dining area is provided shall have an area of not less than 5.0 m² and a minimum width of 1.8 m. The kitchen height (12.3.1) is not less than 2.75 m, the same as a habitable room. Checked on page 44 of Part 3.

12.4.1 Height of bathrooms and water-closets
In NBC 2016, the height of a bathroom, toilet or water-closet (WC), measured from the surface of the floor to the lowest point of the ceiling (bottom of the slab), shall be not less than 2.1 m. This is lower than the 2.75 m required for habitable rooms. Checked on page 44 of Part 3.

12.4.2 Size of bathrooms and water-closets
In NBC 2016, a bathroom shall have an area of not less than 1.8 m² with a minimum width of 1.2 m, and a water-closet (WC, toilet) shall have a floor area of 1.1 m² with a minimum width of 0.9 m. A combined bath and water-closet has its own larger minimum. Checked on page 44 of Part 3.

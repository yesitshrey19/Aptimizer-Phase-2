IS 875 (Part 2):1987 — Imposed Loads (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from IS 875 (Part 2):1987, checked by Saurav Ambasta in Phase II testing against the PDF copy of the standard. Page numbers are PDF page numbers of that copy. This is not the text of the Code.

Table 1 Imposed floor loads for residential buildings
Under IS 875 (Part 2):1987 Table 1, the uniformly distributed imposed floor load in dwellings is 2.0 kN/m² for all rooms and kitchens, and 3.0 kN/m² for staircases and corridors. Checked on PDF page 11.

4.1 Imposed loads on roofs
Under IS 875 (Part 2):1987 Cl. 4.1 and Table 2, a flat roof with access provided carries an imposed load of 1.5 kN/m², and a flat roof without access (other than for maintenance) 0.75 kN/m². Checked on PDF page 18.

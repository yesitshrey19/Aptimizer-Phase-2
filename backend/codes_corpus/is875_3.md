IS 875 (Part 3):2015 — Wind Loads (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from IS 875 (Part 3):2015, checked by Saurav Ambasta in Phase II testing against the PDF copy of the standard. Page numbers are PDF page numbers of that copy. This is not the text of the Code.

Annex A Basic wind speed at 10 m height for some important cities and towns
Under IS 875 (Part 3):2015 Annex A, the basic wind speed Vb is 33 m/s for Bengaluru, 50 m/s for Chennai, 44 m/s for Mumbai and 47 m/s for Delhi. Checked on PDF page 54.

6.3 Design wind speed
Under IS 875 (Part 3):2015 Cl. 6.3, the design wind speed is Vz = Vb × k1 × k2 × k3 × k4, where k1 is the risk coefficient, k2 the terrain roughness and height factor, k3 the topography factor and k4 the importance factor for the cyclonic region. Checked on PDF page 8.

6.3.2.1 Terrain categories
IS 875 (Part 3):2015 Cl. 6.3.2.1 defines four terrain categories, from category 1 (exposed open terrain) to category 4 (terrain with numerous large, closely spaced tall obstructions). Checked on PDF page 8.

Table 1 Risk coefficient k1
Under IS 875 (Part 3):2015 Table 1, the risk coefficient k1 is 1.0 for all general buildings and structures with a design life of 50 years. Checked on PDF page 10.

6.3.4 Importance factor for cyclonic region k4
Under IS 875 (Part 3):2015 Cl. 6.3.4, k4 is 1.0 for ordinary buildings, 1.30 for structures of post-cyclone importance and 1.15 for industrial structures. Checked on PDF page 12.

7.2 Design wind pressure
Under IS 875 (Part 3):2015 Cl. 7.2, the wind pressure at height z is pz = 0.6 Vz² N/m², with Vz the design wind speed at height z in m/s (from Cl. 6.3), giving the design wind pressure on the building at that height. Checked on PDF page 12.

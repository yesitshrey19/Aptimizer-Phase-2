7.2 Minimum depth of foundation
Under IS 1904:1986 Cl. 7.2, all foundations shall extend to a depth of at least 0.50 m below natural ground level. This is a minimum for every foundation; the actual depth also depends on the bearing stratum, scour, frost and movement of the soil. Checked on printed page 10 (PDF page 14).

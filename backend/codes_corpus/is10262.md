IS 10262:2019 — Concrete Mix Proportioning, Guidelines (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from IS 10262:2019, checked by Saurav Ambasta in Phase II testing against the PDF copy of the standard. Page numbers are PDF page numbers of that copy. This is not the text of the Code.

4.2 Target strength for mix proportioning
Under IS 10262:2019 Cl. 4.2, the target mean compressive strength is f'ck = fck + 1.65 S or f'ck = fck + X, whichever is higher, where S is the standard deviation and X a factor by grade (5.5 for M25). For M25 with S = 4.0 N/mm² this gives 25 + 1.65 × 4 = 31.6 N/mm², which is higher than 25 + 5.5. Checked on PDF pages 4 to 5.

Table 2 Assumed standard deviation
Under IS 10262:2019 Table 2, the assumed standard deviation is 4.0 N/mm² for M20 and M25 concrete. Checked on PDF pages 4 to 5.

Table 3 Approximate air content
Under IS 10262:2019 Table 3, the entrapped air content for 20 mm nominal maximum size aggregate is 1.0 percent. Checked on PDF page 5.

Table 4 Maximum water content per cubic metre of concrete
Under IS 10262:2019 Table 4, for a 50 mm slump the maximum water content is 186 kg/m³ for 20 mm nominal maximum size aggregate and 200 kg/m³ for 10 mm aggregate. Checked on PDF page 10.

5.3 Adjustment of water content for slump
Under IS 10262:2019 Cl. 5.3, the water content is increased by about 3 percent for every additional 25 mm of slump above 50 mm, or decreased by the same proportion for a lower slump; the adjustment can also be established by trial batches with the actual materials. Checked on PDF page 7.

Table 5 Volume of coarse aggregate per unit volume of total aggregate
Under IS 10262:2019 Table 5, for 20 mm nominal maximum size aggregate with Zone II fine aggregate at a water-cement ratio of 0.50, the volume of coarse aggregate is 0.62 of the total aggregate volume. Checked on PDF page 8.

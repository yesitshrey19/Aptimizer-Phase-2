IS 875 (Part 1):1987 — Dead Loads, Unit Weights of Building Materials (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from IS 875 (Part 1):1987, checked by Saurav Ambasta in Phase II testing against the PDF copy of the standard. Page numbers are PDF page numbers of that copy. This is not the text of the Code.

Table 1 Unit weight of reinforced cement concrete
Under IS 875 (Part 1):1987 Table 1 (item 'Cement concrete, reinforced'), the unit weight of reinforced concrete depends on the steel content and lies between 22.75 and 26.50 kN/m³: about 22.75 to 24.20 kN/m³ with 1 percent steel, 23.25 to 24.80 kN/m³ with 2 percent steel and 24.80 to 26.50 kN/m³ with 5 percent steel. The commonly used 25 kN/m³ falls within this range but the table does not state it as a single value. Checked on PDF page 13.

IS 1172:1993 — Basic Requirements for Water Supply, Drainage and Sanitation (checked facts)
NOTE — Values summarised in Aptimizer's own words from IS 1172:1993, checked by Saurav Ambasta in Phase II testing against the PDF copy of the standard. Page numbers are printed page numbers. This is not the text of the Code.

4.1 Water supply for residences
Under IS 1172:1993 Cl. 4.1, the domestic water supply for communities with a population of 20,000 to 100,000 with full flushing systems is 100 to 150 litres per head per day; 150 to 200 litres per head per day applies only to communities above 100,000. The 150 to 200 litres per head per day may be reduced to 135 litres per head per day for houses for Lower Income Groups (LIG) and the Economically Weaker Section (EWS), depending on prevailing conditions. Checked on printed page 2 (PDF page 6).

4.1.1 Water for flushing
Under IS 1172:1993 Cl. 4.1.1, out of the 150 to 200 litres per head per day, 45 litres per head per day may be taken for flushing of water-closets; the rest of the domestic supply covers drinking, cooking, bathing, washing and other household uses. Checked on printed page 2 (PDF page 6).
